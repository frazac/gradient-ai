1. How we ask you to give credit

The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence asks you to name the authors and the source "in any reasonable manner" for the medium you use. A badge alone is often not enough: it is small, it is easy to cut out, and the licence line on its edge disappears when the badge is made smaller or when it is removed. So we ask you to add a credit line in text each time you use the levels or the badges, that readers can find and copy. For us, this is the reasonable way to follow the licence.

  • The short credit is enough: "Gradient AI, levels of AI use (getgradient.it)".
  • It does not need to be next to the badge. It can be anywhere in the work where people can read it, for example in the credits, the notes, the footer or the first page. One credit line is enough for all the badges in the same work.
  • If the medium has no place for text (for example an image published alone), put it in the caption, the description or the metadata, or add a link to getgradient.it.
  • If you use the badges or icons without the licence line, or if you change the texts or the badges, use the full credit from How to give credit, and say what you changed.
