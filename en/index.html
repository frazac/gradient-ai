    Gradient AI

    Levels of AI use, based on the AIAS

    Introduction

    Five gradients to show how much AI models are part of a piece of work, a project, a task or a simple text. On this site you can also change these badges. You can choose a context, for example teaching, with a different version for each kind of study. You can also change how they look: their colours, weight and size.

    This page is in Simple English. There is already a better English text: the original AI Assessment Scale (AIAS) by Mike Perkins, Leon Furze, Jasper Roe and Jason MacVaugh. Read it first if English is easy for you.

    Gradient AI started in Italian. We wrote this English version for people who are still learning English. We follow the plain language standard ISO 24495-1:2023 and the rules of the Simple English Wikipedia: common words, short sentences, one idea in each sentence.

    Gradient 1On your own

    On your own

    The work is done with no AI at all. Use this level when you need the person's own skill, when the material is private, or when the client asks for it.

    Examples

    • Expert reports and opinions that a person signs with their own name.
    • Work on private papers that must not leave the organisation.
    • Job interviews and skill tests.

    Gradient AI · Level 1 · On your own

    [Gradient AI · Level 1 · On your own](https://frazac.github.io/gradient-ai/en/#livello-1)

    For the person who does the work (Profile: General, change)

    Do not use AI tools at any step. This includes checking, summing up or translating. If you think a step needs AI, ask first.

    Gradient 2Ideas

    Ideas

    AI can help only before the real work starts: to find ideas, to plan the structure, to learn about a new subject. The person writes and makes the final work.

    Examples

    • Ideas for an offer, a proposal or a new concept.
    • A plan for a report or a talk.
    • A first look at a market, a law or a new subject. Check everything after.

    Gradient AI · Level 2 · Ideas

    [Gradient AI · Level 2 · Ideas](https://frazac.github.io/gradient-ai/en/#livello-2)

    For the person who does the work (Profile: General, change)

    You can use AI to find ideas, to plan and to do first research. You make and write the final work yourself. Check every fact in a trusted source.

    Gradient 3Working together

    Working together

    AI can help to make the work: first drafts, changes, comments, final touches. The person checks, corrects and adds to everything the AI gives. They are responsible for all of it.

    Examples

    • Texts, emails and documents written with an AI assistant, then checked.
    • Code written with an AI assistant, then reviewed and tested.
    • Translations or summaries made by AI, then checked against the original.

    Gradient AI · Level 3 · Working together

    [Gradient AI · Level 3 · Working together](https://frazac.github.io/gradient-ai/en/#livello-3)

    For the person who does the work (Profile: General, change)

    You can use AI for some clear parts of the work, like a first draft, a review or a check. Read, correct and complete everything it makes. You are responsible for the result. Say where you used AI.

    Gradient 4Directing

    Directing

    AI can be used for any part of the work, or the work needs AI. The value is in the direction: clear goals, exact instructions, quality checks and good reasons for each choice.

    Examples

    • Data or documents studied with AI agents, with spot checks.
    • A first version of a product or service made with AI tools.
    • Many texts or images made with AI, following clear style rules.

    Gradient AI · Level 4 · Directing

    [Gradient AI · Level 4 · Directing](https://frazac.github.io/gradient-ai/en/#livello-4)

    For the person who does the work (Profile: General, change)

    You can use AI a lot. What matters is how you lead it to the goal, how you check the results and how you explain your choices. Keep notes on how you worked.

    Gradient 5Exploring

    Exploring

    AI is the heart of the work. People try new ways to use it: to solve problems, to find new ideas or to build new solutions. The client and the team agree on the goals and the method together.

    Examples

    • A small test project to bring AI into a way of working.
    • A tool made to measure with AI models.
    • Research on new services that use AI.

    Gradient AI · Level 5 · Exploring

    [Gradient AI · Level 5 · Exploring](https://frazac.github.io/gradient-ai/en/#livello-5)

    For the person who does the work (Profile: General, change)

    Use AI in creative and new ways. Suggest new ideas, agree on them with the client, and write down what works and what does not.

    How to use the badges

    There are four ways, from the easiest to the most flexible. The examples use level 3 and link to its part of this page. Shape, colours and weight follow your choices in the panel above.

    1. Text only, with a link

    For documents, emails, learning platforms and websites: one line that gives the level and links to it. The button “Copy text with link” under each level copies this line for you. In Word, Google Docs or an email, the link still works when you paste it.

    In HTML:

    2. PNG image

    For slides, documents and platforms that only take images. Each stamp comes at 128 and 512 px (pictogram: 64 and 256; label: 48 and 192 px high), as an outline or filled.

    All PNG files

    3. SVG file

    A vector file: it stays sharp at any size, and you can change it in Illustrator, Inkscape or Figma. The text uses a system font.

    All SVG files

    4. JavaScript

    You mark the place with an attribute, and the library draws the badge, with the colours and weight you want.

    Options: variant (stamp, icon, label) · lang (en or it) · color · from/to (a gradient in five steps) · palette (five colours) · weight (1–3) · filled · size (px) · link (by default the badge links to its level on this site; false removes the link, or give the address of a profile). To get only the SVG text: GradientAI.toSvg(3, {variant: 'stamp', lang: 'en'}).

    Notes on use

    • The general profile takes a scale made for schools and universities and uses it for work. This is our own addition. It is not part of the original AIAS.
    • The levels do not say how good the work is. They only say how much AI is part of it, and how.
    • Agree on the level before the work starts. Write it down, for example in a brief, a contract or a team rule. Also agree on what people must report, and who is responsible for the result.
    • You can only be sure of level 1 when you can see how the work is done. At other levels you trust what people tell you. It helps to ask them to keep notes on how they worked.

    How this project used AI

    Content

    The author made the adaptation and the main choices: the names of the levels, the profiles, and the focus on work and on teaching. The texts were written together with AI (Claude), following the author's directions. The author reads, corrects and checks them against the AIAS v2.

    Colours

    AI suggested a first set of ten colours and made a tool to adjust them. It also measured the contrast and the difference between the colours. The author chose the direction and adjusted the colours by eye. AI chose some of the final colours, following the author's directions.

    Website, code and design

    The website, the JavaScript library, the build tools, the badges and the GitHub repository were made with Claude Code. The author set the goals and the design choices, and checked and tested the result.

    Made with Claude, the AI model by Anthropic, through Claude Code (September 2026).

    Host a research project

    Gradient AI can be studied in real use. For example: how teachers, students and clients read the levels, if the levels help people say how they used AI, and where the line between two levels is not clear. Do you work at a university, a department or a research centre? Do you want to host research on this?
