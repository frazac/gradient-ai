    Gradient AI · STEM teaching

    The five levels, ready to copy: a line with a link to the level, and the text for the person who does the work.

    Teaching profile · STEM

    General STEM teaching Humanities teaching Arts teaching

    Gradient 1On your own

    On your own (no AI)

    Gradient AI · Level 1 · On your own (no AI) — https://getgradient.it/en/stem/#livello-1

    For students (Profile: STEM teaching, change)

    Do not use AI at any step. This task shows what you know and what you can do on your own. Show all your steps.

    Gradient 2Ideas

    Ideas

    Gradient AI · Level 2 · Ideas — https://getgradient.it/en/stem/#livello-2

    For students (Profile: STEM teaching, change)

    You can use AI to find ways to solve the problem and to plan your project. You do the maths, the code and the development yourself.

    Gradient 3Working together

    Working together

    Gradient AI · Level 3 · Working together — https://getgradient.it/en/stem/#livello-3

    For students (Profile: STEM teaching, change)

    You can use AI for some parts of the task. Check every result. You must be able to explain every line of code. Say how you used AI.

    Gradient 4Directing

    Directing

    Gradient AI · Level 4 · Directing — https://getgradient.it/en/stem/#livello-4

    For students (Profile: STEM teaching, change)

    You can use AI a lot, as you like or as the task says. What matters is how you lead it and how you check what it makes.

    Gradient 5Exploring

    Exploring

    Gradient AI · Level 5 · Exploring — https://getgradient.it/en/stem/#livello-5

    For students (Profile: STEM teaching, change)

    Use AI in creative ways to do the task. You can plan new methods together with your teacher.
